Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Lintel Enterprise OPC handles personal information in connection with our websites, software, and WhatsApp-enabled CRM services.

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Important: Where Lintel provides the CRM as a service to a clinic, healthcare organization, or other business, that organization may determine the purposes for which customer or patient information is processed. In those circumstances, Lintel may act as a personal information processor/service provider on the organization's behalf. The organization may have its own privacy notice that also applies to your information.

1. Who We Are

Lintel Enterprise OPC develops and provides software and technology services, including business applications and WhatsApp-enabled customer communication and CRM solutions.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at info@lintel.com.ph.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to personal information processed through our websites, applications, CRM services, customer support channels, and related technology services.

It covers information received through WhatsApp and other communication channels when those channels are connected to a Lintel-managed application.

3. Personal Information We May Process

Depending on the service and how it is configured by the business using it, we may process:

  • Name and contact information, such as telephone number, email address, and other details provided by the user.
  • WhatsApp account and message information, including message content, timestamps, message identifiers, delivery status, read status, attachments, and related conversation metadata.
  • CRM information such as conversation assignments, tags, notes, appointment information, and customer-service history.
  • Account information for authorized CRM users, such as username, role, organization, authentication information, and audit activity.
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser or device information, application logs, and security events where necessary to operate and secure the service.

Messages sent by users may contain information they choose to provide about themselves, including potentially sensitive or health-related information. Lintel does not require users to send medical information through WhatsApp unless the relevant service or organization specifically provides for that communication.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We process personal information only for specified and legitimate purposes, which may include:

  • Providing, operating, maintaining, and securing the CRM and related services.
  • Receiving, storing, routing, displaying, and sending WhatsApp messages on behalf of an authorized business or clinic.
  • Managing customer support conversations, assignments, follow-ups, and appointments where those functions are enabled.
  • Authenticating authorized users and enforcing access permissions.
  • Detecting fraud, abuse, security incidents, and unauthorized access.
  • Complying with legal obligations and responding to lawful requests.
  • Improving the reliability and performance of our services using appropriate safeguards and, where practicable, aggregated or de-identified information.

5. Lawful Basis and Data Privacy Principles

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and its implementing rules, processing must follow the principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality. Depending on the specific processing activity, an applicable lawful basis may include consent, contractual necessity, compliance with a legal obligation, or another basis permitted by applicable law.

We aim to collect and process only information that is adequate, relevant, and necessary for the purpose for which it is processed. Personal information is not retained longer than necessary for the declared purpose, legal requirements, legitimate business purposes, or the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims, as applicable.

6. WhatsApp and Third-Party Services

Our CRM may integrate with WhatsApp Business Platform / WhatsApp Cloud API and other third-party infrastructure providers.

When a WhatsApp conversation is handled through our CRM, information may be transmitted between WhatsApp, the business operating the WhatsApp account, and our systems in order to deliver the requested service. Those third parties may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not sell personal information to third parties for their independent advertising purposes.

7. Disclosure and Sharing

Personal information may be accessible to:

  • The business, clinic, or organization that uses the CRM and its authorized personnel.
  • Service providers that support hosting, infrastructure, security, communications, backups, and other functions necessary to operate the service.
  • Third parties where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law, legal process, or lawful government authority.

Where Lintel acts as a processor on behalf of a business, that business remains responsible for determining the purposes and authorized scope of the relevant processing, subject to applicable law.

8. Security

We implement reasonable and appropriate organizational, physical, and technical measures intended to protect personal information against accidental or unlawful destruction, alteration, loss, unauthorized access, disclosure, or other unlawful processing.

Depending on the service, safeguards may include role-based access control, authentication controls, encrypted connections, secrets management, audit logging, monitoring, secure backups, vulnerability management, and restricted administrative access.

No system or method of transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore continuously review and improve our security practices based on the nature and risks of the information we process.

9. Retention and Secure Disposal

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for applicable contractual or legitimate business needs, for legal or regulatory requirements, or for the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete, destroy, anonymize, or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable requirements and our contractual obligations.

Where Lintel processes information on behalf of a client organization, retention and deletion may also be controlled by that organization's instructions and applicable law.

10. Data Subject Rights

Subject to the conditions and limitations provided by applicable law, data subjects may have rights including the right to be informed, access, correction or rectification, objection, erasure or blocking where warranted, data portability, and the right to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Requests should be made using the contact information below. We may need to verify the identity and authority of the requester before processing a request, and certain requests may be subject to lawful exceptions or limitations.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our websites or applications may use cookies, local storage, session identifiers, and similar technologies for functions such as authentication, security, preferences, performance, and service operation.

Where optional analytics or other non-essential technologies are used, the applicable service may provide additional controls or notices.

12. International Data Transfers

Some infrastructure or service providers we use may process information outside the Philippines. Where personal information is transferred or made accessible across borders, we will apply appropriate contractual, organizational, and technical safeguards consistent with applicable privacy requirements.

13. Children's Privacy

Our business services are not directed to children for independent account creation unless specifically stated by the applicable service. We do not knowingly solicit unnecessary personal information from children. Where a client organization provides services involving minors, the applicable organization is responsible for ensuring that collection and processing are conducted in accordance with applicable law and its privacy obligations.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, processing activities, legal obligations, or security practices change. We will update the effective date when changes are made. Where required, we will provide additional notice for material changes.
